# Is Transport on Track for 1.5°C?

Interactive digital brochure summarising seven key findings from the GIZ and SLOCAT analysis of transport commitments in third-generation Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) under the Paris Agreement.

Covers 126 active third-generation NDCs submitted as of September 2026.

## What it covers

1. Transport presence in third-generation NDCs
2. Target quality and ambition
3. The geographic gap between targets and emissions
4. Electrification as the dominant but underspecified action
5. Freight as a blind spot
6. Social inclusion gaps
7. Adaptation commitments

## How to use

Open `transport-ndc-brochure.html` in any modern browser. No server or installation needed.

Navigate with the scroll rail on the right, arrow keys, or by scrolling. Each page carries one finding.

Use the **Page** or **Full PDF** buttons in the top right to export. Output is a horizontal 16:9 PDF ready to share or present.

## Data sources

- GIZ-SLOCAT NDC Transport Tracker (August 2026 export)
- EDGAR 2024 (transport emissions reference)
